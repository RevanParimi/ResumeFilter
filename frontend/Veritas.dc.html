What happens next

Each resume is extracted, claim-checked and scored across four subsystems, one small group at a time. It is slow on purpose, and it runs here rather than on a server: leave the page and the batch pauses where it is, come back and it carries on. Results land on the screening queue as they finish.

One unreadable file refuses the whole batch by design — a half-registered upload leaves you unable to say which files made it in.
